### Topic 1: The Knowledge Assessment (Live Exam)

#### Detailed Definition and Introduction
The assessment is not designed to trick you. It is designed to validate that you can safely and effectively operate within the DWIHN AI ecosystem. Passing this exam (80% or higher) grants you official certification, which may be required to maintain your access to Enterprise AI tools.

#### Required Setup/Tools
* Laptops or tablets connected to the internet.
* Access to the LMS (Learning Management System) where the digital exam is hosted (or physical paper copies if preferred).

#### The Assessment Protocol
The assessment tests understanding of the core concepts covered over the past 8 weeks. It is open-note, allowing use of the Quick-Reference Desk Cards (The Wallet Analogy) and any notes taken.

### Topic 2: Reviewing the Answers and Addressing Final Doubts

#### Why this Concept is Needed
Taking a test is only half the learning process. Immediate feedback is required to correct any misconceptions before staff return to their desks.

#### Knowledge Validation and Review
It is critical to review missed concepts immediately following the assessment to correct misconceptions.

#### Addressing Common Misconceptions
* **Scenario:** Misunderstanding data de-identification (e.g., removing just the last name).
* **Correction:** HIPAA defines 18 identifiers. Even without a name, a zip code combined with a specific date of service can identify a member. This is why public tools must *never* be used for member data.
